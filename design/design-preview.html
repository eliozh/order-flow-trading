08

Direction rules

What this system is, and what it refuses to become

Do

  • Use near-black #0A0B0D, 1px grid, and 0-radius panels.
  • Keep green/red for market meaning only; use cyan for interaction and LLM provenance.
  • Set all numeric data in mono with tabular-nums slashed-zero.
  • Show data quality and provenance on every surface that uses derived data.
  • Show partial data with N/A/STALE instead of hiding the screen.
  • Encode volume direction by shape too: up = top cap, down = outline.
  • Keep the kill switch available in every state, including errors and mobile.

Don't

  • Purple-to-blue gradients, gradient text, or decorative glow.
  • Glassmorphism, blurred backdrops, translucent floating cards.
  • Rounded cards, nested cards, oversized rounded stat tiles.
  • Emoji as status icons or exclamation-mark error copy.
  • Rainbow heat scales or any color without a stated meaning.
  • Marketing hero sections in an operational terminal.
